# Closed-Loop Revenue Analytics & Business Intelligence

## 1. Overview & Methodological Foundations

RecoverAI's Closed-Loop Analytics Engine provides mathematically rigorous, database-backed business intelligence for Track 03 of the Razorpay Ideathon (*"AI Revenue Recovery"*).

Unlike traditional dashboards that display speculative predictions or theoretical win-backs, **RecoverAI computes all monetary metrics strictly from persisted `RecoveryOutcome` records created by actual execution simulator events**.

---

## 2. Key Business Metrics & Explicit Formulas

| Metric | Formula | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Payment Volume (TPV)** | $\sum \text{Transaction.amount}$ | Total transaction volume across the merchant portfolio. |
| **Failed Payment Value** | $\sum \text{Transaction.amount} \quad [\text{status} \neq \text{CAPTURED}]$ | Monetary value of all historical payment failures. |
| **Revenue at Risk** | $\sum \text{RecoveryCase.revenue\_at\_risk} \quad [\text{status} \in \{\text{ELIGIBLE}, \text{IN\_PROGRESS}\}]$ | Active unrecovered payment value currently open for intervention. |
| **Actual Recovered Revenue** | $\sum \text{RecoveryOutcome.recovered\_amount} \quad [\text{result} = \text{SUCCESS}]$ | Total money won back through autonomous recovery interventions. |
| **Baseline Recovered Revenue** | $\sum \text{RecoveryOutcome.baseline\_amount} \quad [\text{result} = \text{SUCCESS}]$ | Counterfactual revenue that would have recovered organically without agent intervention. |
| **Incremental Recovered Revenue** | $\max(0, \text{Actual Recovered Revenue} - \text{Baseline Recovered Revenue})$ | **True incremental revenue** uniquely generated by the AI recovery agent. |
| **Incremental Lift** | $\frac{\text{Incremental Recovered Revenue}}{\text{Baseline Recovered Revenue}}$ (or $0.0$ if denominator is $0$) | Percentage multiplier of financial lift over organic baseline. |
| **Agent Recovery Rate** | $\frac{\text{Successful Recovery Cases}}{\text{Total Recovery Cases}}$ (or $0.0$ if denominator is $0$) | Proportion of recovery cases converted to `RECOVERED`. |
| **Policy Block Rate** | $\frac{\text{Policy Rejections}}{\text{Total Evaluated Actions}}$ (or $0.0$ if denominator is $0$) | Percentage of proposed actions blocked by safety guardrails. |
| **Execution Success Rate** | $\frac{\text{Successful Recoveries}}{\text{Actions Executed}}$ (or $0.0$ if denominator is $0$) | Operational reliability of approved actions at execution time. |

*Zero-Denominator Protection: Every formula explicitly checks for $0$ denominators and returns $0.0$ rather than `NaN` or `Infinity`.*

## 3. Closed-Loop Recovery Funnel

The 8-stage recovery funnel tracks each stage of the recovery lifecycle directly against persisted database records:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Failed Payments} &\longrightarrow \text{Recovery Eligible} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{ML High Recovery Likelihood} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{AI Recommended} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{Policy Approved} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{Action Executed} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{Actual Recovery} \\
&\longrightarrow \text{Incremental Lift}
\end{aligned}$$

## 4. Analytical Breakdowns & Intelligence

### 4.1. By Failure Taxonomy (6 Canonical Types)
- `TEMPORARY_BANK_DECLINE` (Highest recovery rate with delayed intelligent retries)
- `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` (Optimal with scheduled payday reminders & smart links)
- `BANK_DECLINE` (Route alternative payment method)
- `NETWORK_ERROR` (Immediate automated retry)

## 5. Predicted vs. Actual Calibration

The system groups transactions into probability buckets ($[0.00-0.30), [0.30-0.60), [0.60-0.80), [0.80-1.00]$) and measures:
- **Predicted Expected Recovery**: $\sum (P(\text{Recovery}) \times \text{amount})$
- **Actual Recovered Revenue**: $\sum \text{outcome.recovered\_amount}$
- **Prediction-to-Actual Ratio**: $\frac{\text{Actual Recovered}}{\text{Predicted Expected}}$

*Model Performance Disclosure: The recovery model is trained and calibrated on resolved synthetic transaction populations. While highly effective in simulation, real-world deployment requires continuous holdout A/B testing.*

---

## 6. Systemic Incident & Circuit-Breaker Analytics

Tracks correlated gateway/issuer outage events:
- **Incident Detection**: $\text{Failure Spike Rate} \ge 0.25$ (configurable simulation threshold).
- **Circuit Breaker**: Automatically blocks automated payment retries to prevent gateway throttling and cascading retries.
- **Impact Tracking**: Counts affected transactions and blocked retries in real-time.
